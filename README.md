# Vorian scheduler portal (demo)

Three tabs.

1. Weekly schedule: a charter operator's week as the scheduler built it, with any trip that breaks a rule flagged. Generate a random week swaps in a different made-up week.
2. Vorian's review: first the week as built, with the rule problems Vorian found; then Vorian's plan, with a reason for each change, approve or keep current, and a sick call that Vorian re-plans around.
3. Plan a month: 30 days of made-up trips, planned by Vorian three days at a time and then checked again as one schedule, with the time it took.

When no legal crew is left for a trip, Vorian leaves it uncovered with the reason instead of breaking a rule, and the scheduler decides: subcharter it, call in a reserve or contract pilot, or ask the client to move the time.

Every result on this page was computed ahead of time by Vorian's scheduler and legality checker, so the page needs no server; planning times were measured when the page was built. Made-up data. Vorian suggests; the operator's scheduler approves every change and keeps operational control (14 CFR 135.77).
